# Expense Management

## Goal
Verify the ability to review and modify recorded expenses.

## Test Cases
1. **View Details**: Verify that clicking an expense opens a detail view showing the total, the payer, and the individual splits.
2. **Edit Expense**: Verify that changing the amount or the split of an existing expense updates the record.
3. **Delete Expense**: Verify that deleting an expense removes it from the history and updates outstanding debts.
