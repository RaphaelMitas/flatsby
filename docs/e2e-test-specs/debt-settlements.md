# Debt & Settlements

## Goal
Verify the debt summary and the process of settling up.

## Test Cases
1. **Debt Summary**: Verify that the debts page correctly displays who owes whom and the total amount.
2. **Record Settlement**: Verify that creating a "Settlement" payment reduces or clears the debt between two users.
3. **Zero Balance State**: Verify the UI when all debts are settled ("All settled up").
