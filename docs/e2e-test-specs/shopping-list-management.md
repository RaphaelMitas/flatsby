# Shopping List Management

## Goal
Verify high-level management of the lists themselves.

## Test Cases
1. **Rename List**: Verify that a user can rename a list from its "⋯" in the switcher.
2. **Delete List**: Verify that deleting the open list from the switcher removes it with its items and opens another list.
3. **Category Filtering**: Verify that selecting a specific category filter only shows items belonging to that category.
